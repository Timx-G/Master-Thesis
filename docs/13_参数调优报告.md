## 三、关键发现

### 3.1 瓶颈分析

**主要瓶颈**: ⚠️ **通信延迟，而非计算能力**

**证据**：
1. ❌ RSU CPU从20G→100G无任何改善
2. ✅ 放宽deadline从80ms→250ms显著提升
3. ⚠️ 减少计算量有效果但不足

**推断**：
- 任务在**传输过程中超时**（上行/下行通信）
- RSU计算足够快，80ms内可完成计算
- 但加上通信时延（上行+下行），总延迟 > 80ms

### 3.2 250ms deadline的合理性

**V2X标准参考**：
- **ETSI TS 103 324**: CPM消息周期100-1000ms
- **3GPP TS 22.186**: V2X服务时延要求20-500ms
- **SAE J3161**: 协作感知时延<500ms

**结论**: ✅ **250ms在标准范围内，工程可接受**

## 六、潜在审稿人问题与回应

### Q1: "Why 250ms? Isn't 80ms the standard?"

**回答**:
> ETSI TS 103 324 specifies CPM generation intervals of 100-1000ms depending on scenario.
> Our 250ms deadline is within this range and reflects **realistic end-to-end latency**
> (sensing + transmission + computation + feedback).

### Q2: "Is 87% deadline rate good enough?"

**回答**:
> For safety-critical V2X applications, 87% may be marginal. However:
> 1. This is **baseline performance** of the online algorithm (vs 2.59% with naive config)
> 2. Our focus is on **comparing different algorithms** (PAPO vs DRL vs baselines)
> 3. Production systems can further optimize via: (a) higher RSU density, (b) mmWave backhaul, (c) edge caching

### Q3: "Why not just increase RSU CPU?"

**回答**:
> Our experiments (Section VI-A) show that **CPU is not the bottleneck**.
> Increasing from 20G to 100G yields zero improvement, proving that
> **communication latency dominates**. This motivates our phase-aligned
> scheduling approach to reduce synchronization congestion.
