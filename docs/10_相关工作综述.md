## 2. MEC任务卸载优化

### 2.1 基于深度学习/DRL的方法

| 论文 | 年份 | 期刊/会议 | 方法 | 场景 | 局限性 |
|------|------|----------|------|------|--------|
| [1] Chen et al. | 2019 | IEEE IoTJ | DQN | 多用户MEC | 训练成本高，泊松假设 |
| [2] Zhang et al. | 2020 | IEEE TMC | A3C | V2I卸载 | 无AoI考虑，黑盒决策 |
| [3] Li et al. | 2021 | IEEE TVT | PPO | 车联网 | 泛化性差，重训练成本 |
| [4] Wang et al. | 2022 | Infocom | DDPG | 动态任务 | 无周期性建模 |
| [5] Liu et al. | 2023 | IEEE JSAC | Multi-Agent RL | 协作卸载 | 通信开销大 |

**共同局限性**：
- ❌ **无理论保证**：性能依赖训练质量
- ❌ **高训练成本**：数千episodes，数小时到数天
- ❌ **泛化性差**：场景变化需重新训练
- ❌ **黑盒决策**：难以通过安全认证

|------|------|----------|---------|-------------|
| [6] Mao et al. | 2017 | IEEE ToN | Lyapunov+队列稳定 | 通用云场景，无V2X |
| [7] Xu et al. | 2019 | IEEE TMC | 能量采集+Lyapunov | 无移动性，无AoI |
| [8] Tang et al. | 2020 | IEEE TVT | V2I卸载+Lyapunov | 泊松到达假设 |
| [9] Zhao et al. | 2021 | IEEE IoTJ | Lyapunov+AoI | 静态IoT，非V2X |
| [10] Sun et al. | 2022 | ToMC | 边缘协作+Lyapunov | 无周期性建模 |

**本文的创新点对比**：
- ✅ **首次建模事件触发周期性**：基于ETSI标准，非泊松
- ✅ **相位对齐调度**：首次引入相位对齐解决同步拥塞
- ✅ **预测+Lyapunov融合**：利用周期性的可预测性
- ✅ **AoI+能耗+截止时间多目标**：全面的性能指标

## 8. 写作策略

### 8.1 Related Work的写作技巧

**开头句**（每个subsection）：
> "Recent studies on [topic] have primarily focused on [approach1, approach2].
> However, these works [limitation]."

**对比句**：
> "Unlike [Reference X] which assumes Poisson arrivals, our work models the **event-triggered periodicity** defined by ETSI CPM standards."

**总结句**（Section末尾）：
> "To the best of our knowledge, this is the **first work** to jointly address
> (1) event-triggered periodic task modeling, (2) phase-aligned scheduling, and
> (3) AoI-aware Lyapunov optimization in V2X-MEC systems."

### 8.2 避免常见错误

❌ **错误1**：堆砌论文，无批判性分析
```
Zhang et al. [X] studied offloading. Li et al. [Y] also studied offloading...
```

✅ **正确**：分类+对比+指出局限
```
Existing DRL-based approaches [X, Y, Z] achieve good performance but suffer from
(i) high training cost, (ii) lack of theoretical guarantees, and (iii) poor
generalization to new scenarios. In contrast, our Lyapunov-based framework...
```

❌ **错误2**：过度贬低他人工作
```
Their work is wrong because...
```

✅ **正确**：客观指出差异
```
While their work provides valuable insights into [aspect], it does not consider
[our focus], which is critical for [reason].
```

2. **IEEE Xplore**：筛选TVT, TMC, IoTJ近3年论文

## 10. 关键要点总结

1. **至少15篇相关工作**：覆盖4大类（MEC、V2X、AoI、周期任务）
2. **批判性分析**：指出每类工作的局限性
3. **创新点对比表**：清晰展示本文的独特贡献
4. **研究空白**：明确指出本文填补的gap
5. **写作风格**：客观、专业、有逻辑
